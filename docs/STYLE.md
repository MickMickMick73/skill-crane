# Skill Crane — style sheet

Approve this before any sprite. Eight colours, one light, one line weight.

## Palette

| Name | Hex | Use |
|------|-----|-----|
| Pit | `#1c1714` | Inside the cabinet, the well |
| Cabinet | `#2e5a4c` | Worn arcade teal, chipped |
| Lamp | `#ead9b0` | The single bulb over the pit |
| Brass | `#b07a32` | Coins, plaque screws, chute lip |
| Chute | `#8e2a24` | Prize chute felt |
| Glass | `#8ea3aa` | Front pane, a scratch in it |
| Plush | `#c06e6e` | Dusty prize pink, never candy |
| Sign | `#f2ead8` | Hand-lettered cabinet words (in-world only; not in sprites) |

No ninth colour. No glow. No pure white. No cyan.

## Light

One bulb, high and slightly left of centre, warm. The pit is a well: prizes in the back sit in half-shadow. The glass catches a single smear of lamp, not a studio reflection. Night outside the arcade, so the cabinet is the only light in the room.

## Line

Cabinet and claw: a fat, slightly uneven brush, about three pixels at 1x. Prizes: thinner, lumpy, not outlined in black. Scratches on the glass are one-pixel and few. Nothing is symmetrical on purpose.

## What it is not

Not a casino. Not a phone game. Not concept-art chrome. Lettering on the cabinet is signwriting, painted once and faded, never a font. Plush are lumpy. The claw is a bit bent.
